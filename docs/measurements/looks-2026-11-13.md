# How detailed items look: measured 2026-11-13

Phase 0 of the looks revamp. Read only: nothing was written to production and no model was called. Made by `scripts/look-measure.ts`.

## Headline

- 348 library programs and 20 daily wonders (programs in the live pool) measured. The forms list has 486 elements; 82 of the programs read are not in it (form "unsorted" below).
- 61 (16.6%) draw at least one model (w.model, or a creature with a drawn model). 307 draw none.
- **184 (50%) have no look at all**: no model, no creature of their own, no object they declare. They show effects only (particles, terrain).
- Under 10 parts in total: 318 items (86.4% of all, 18% of those that draw anything).
- Single colour (of items that draw a model): 0 (0%). Three colours or fewer: 5 (8.2%). Exactly one box: 0.
- No fine part (nothing under 0.3 on every side): 45.9% of those that draw a model.
- Boxes per model, items that draw one: median 19 (10th 6, 90th 36).

## Method

- **Library.** Read through routes that write nothing (confirmed in `worker/index.ts`: `handleAdmin` "programs" / "challenge-candidates" / "forms" are one SELECT each; `POST /api/library` is `handleLibrary`, one SELECT over `spells` joined to `elements`; `GET /api/wonders` is `livePool`, one SELECT; the admin payer never triggers `seen()`). The owner's word "measure: all" covers reading the whole live library read only.
- **Run.** Each program is cast once at the village edge of the practice island (seed 11, side 112, `src/sim/check.ts` island as `scripts/spell-measure.ts` uses it) in the repository's own `Runtime`, for 30 seconds of game time. Artifacts and wonders are cast as artifacts (a wonder with its own reach).
- **What counts as drawn.** Every distinct model a program passed to `w.model` (readBox, `src/sim/props.ts`, as the island reads them but before it shades each part a little; the island's own limit of four models per placing is not applied, so a program that places many is counted whole, at most 60 distinct models). Plus the raw model of every creature the program declares (`modelOf`, `src/sim/body.ts`, 16 boxes at most), taken before `bodyFrom` turns a still model into a plan.
- **Per item.** models (distinct); parts (boxes over all its models); boxes per model (parts divided by models); fine parts (largest side under 0.3); thin parts (smallest side under 0.3, a page or a blade); distinct colours; glow share (glow boxes over boxes); silhouette score.
- **Silhouette score.** For the item's largest model: the boxes are drawn onto a 24 by 24 grid over the bounding box, seen from the front, the side and the top. For each view the outline is the filled cells with an empty neighbour. A solid rectangle has the outline of the grid's edge (92 cells) and scores 0; three times that scores 1; the three views are averaged. A single brick scores 0; the richest outline in the library scores 0.43. "fill" in the JSON is the share of grid cells filled, for the reader who wants the plain version.
- **No look at all.** No model, no creature of its own, no object it declares (`spell.artifacts`).
- Percentiles are the 10th, the median and the 90th, linear between ranks. Tables by kind use the library's seven kinds (spell, creature, object, building, settlement, place, land) and wonders; tiers are the element's own.
- "with a model" is the share of the group that draws any model; "boxes per model" and "colours" are over those that draw one; "total parts" and "no look" are over the whole group, "silhouette" over those that draw a model.

## What could not be measured, and why

- **220 of 486 elements have no readable program.** The read only routes give code for a limited set: `/api/admin/programs` (100 live programs not judged yet, and 100 with creatures), `/api/admin/challenge-candidates` (60), and `POST /api/library`, which returns spells only (`e.kind = 'spell'`), 20 names at a time. An artifact (object, building, settlement, place, land) outside the first three lists has no read only route to its program. By form: building (artifact) 7, creature (artifact) 88, land (artifact) 12, land (spell) 3, object (artifact) 38, place (artifact) 24, settlement (artifact) 3, spell (artifact) 8, spell (spell) 37. These are a count here, not in any distribution: the distributions describe the 368 programs that could be read, which is not a random sample of the artifacts.
- **Built-in wonders** (the island's own hot spring, arch and the rest) are drawn by the view (`voxel/wonders.ts`), not by a program, and are left out. Only wonders written as programs and live in the pool are in.
- **What a program draws late or by chance.** Each program ran once, 30 seconds, one island, one spot. A model drawn only on water, at night, after a minute, or from a random branch is missed, so the numbers are a floor.
- **Looks that are not models.** Particles, the voxels a program sets, wards, terrain, creature plans (a body word like "four legs") and the artifacts a program declares are not boxes. They are counted as "no look" only when the program has none of them; they are not scored.
- **Programs that did not run clean:** 0 (listed in the JSON under `crashed`). Their numbers are what they drew before stopping.
- **Frames:** a view from the Spell Lab's island (`docs/measurements/looks-2026-11-13/`) is the game's medium island, not the practice island, so frames show the look, not the numbers.
