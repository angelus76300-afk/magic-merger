# The World at 60 frames a second (2026-11-07)

The owner's HUD on a foldable (about 806 by 968 CSS pixels, dpr 2), a large island (208 cells, 193 creatures): frame 33.3 / 33.4 ms (p50 / p95), 29.9 fps, sim 8.7 / 18.8 ms, draw 7.5 / 17.3 ms, 87 calls, 323k triangles.

## Why 33.3 ms flat

A cap, not load. `src/sim/engine.ts` drew 30 frames a second at most on every touch screen (`pointer: coarse`, since 3.3), one per simulation tick (the simulation ticks 30 times a second). The phone had time to spare: 8.7 + 7.5 = 16.2 ms of work at p50 in a 33.3 ms frame. Main reproduces it exactly in a headless Pixel 7 with the drawing stubbed out: 30 fps, 33.3 / 33.4 ms, whatever the work (table below).

## What changed

1. **Pacing** (`src/sim/voxel/pacing.ts`): every device aims for 60 draws a second. Every 90 frames the loop counts the frames that came more than 1.5 vsyncs late; over 12%, it falls back to 30 and tries 60 again after 15 s, then 30 s, up to 4 minutes. A try that holds for a minute resets the wait. The browser gives no GPU time, so the cadence of the frames is the signal.
2. **Creatures between two ticks** (`view.ts`, `placeCreatures`): the frame between two ticks draws each creature part way from where it stood one tick before to where it stands now, with its heading and stride. Without it the extra frames would show the same picture twice. Cost: creatures are drawn one tick (33 ms) behind the simulation; a creature that jumps more than 3 cells is drawn there at once.
3. **The land scanned only after a tick or a cast** (`view.ts`): `terrain.scan()` compared all 43,264 columns of a large island on every frame, 3.5 ms p50 in the Perf Lab. The land only changes in a tick or a cast.
4. **One tick a frame** (`ticksDue`): a second tick runs only when more than one is still owed. On a clean vsync the old loop rarely ran two (0.2 to 1% of frames, replayed in `scripts/pacing-test.ts`), so this is not what the owner's sim p95 is; with time stamps 0.1 ms off it ran two in a third of its frames, at 23 fps.
5. **Pixel density** (`adapt`): at 60, a late cadence falls back to 30 first; only the draw time lowers the pixels there.

All behind `GRAPHICS.smooth` (`src/sim/voxel/graphics.ts`); off is the old pacing, `off=smooth` in the Perf Lab.

## Numbers

Same seed (7), same size (large, 208), Pixel 7 emulation, this container (4 cores, SwiftShader). SwiftShader rasterizes 300k triangles in 130 to 180 ms at any pixel density, so the HUD's frame times here measure the software GPU, not the game: `scripts/perf-phone.mjs` gives 6 to 9 fps before and after alike. The numbers that mean something are the JavaScript per frame and the loop's cadence with the drawing replaced by known work.

Two runs each, raw lines in `pacing-2026-11-07.jsonl` (with a 1.5 s warm up, the 20 ms case's p95 was 49.9: the 90 frames before it fell back). The frame between ticks is a third cheaper (the scan). The frame with a tick is unchanged: it is the simulation plus a full draw.

### The simulation, in Node (`scripts/prof-life.ts`, 60 s)

Tick p50 / p95 / max: 2.2 / 3.4 / 25 ms (the island as a new player gets it), 2.5 / 4.3 / 10 ms with guests. The phone's 8.7 / 18.8 ms is the same tick about three times slower, with its own spikes (garbage collection, the passes that run every few ticks); this change does not touch them.

## What the owner's phone should show

At the owner's p50 (16.2 ms of work in a frame with a tick, 7.5 or less without), the loop holds 60 in the emulation. On the phone, the GPU's time and the tick spikes (p95 18.8 ms) come on top. Expected: 60 a second while looking at the island, an occasional 33 ms frame; if more than 12% of frames come late, the pacer settles at 30 and tries again later, which is no worse than before. The real answer is the HUD on the phone after the deploy. The follow up that removes the tick from the frame is the simulation in a Web Worker (`docs/IDEAS.md`, Performance).

Cost: about twice the draws while the island is on screen, so more battery and heat on phones that hold 60. A hot phone that slows down falls back to 30 on its own.
