- **Rings where a wader walks** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-24; lens: what the player feels, a promise kept by the water). Now that a villager in the shallows stands in the water as drawn, the water could answer: a small pale ring every few steps at its feet, widening and fading over a second, and a slower one round a heron standing still. What the player feels: the water is a thing the figures touch, not a floor painted blue; a ford reads as a ford from the sky. What it adds: life on the lakes and rivers between spells, and a hint of where villagers cross. Cost: no paid calls; one instanced flat ring mesh like the shore's sparks (afloat.ts already gives the height), at most a few dozen on screen, and a check that rings sit within 0.05 of the water like the figures. Claude.
