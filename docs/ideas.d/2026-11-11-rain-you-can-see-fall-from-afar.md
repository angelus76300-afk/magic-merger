- **Rain you can see fall from afar** (Claude, the lead session, while building the fronts of "weather that crosses the island"; lens: feedback). From far away a front is a grey cloud over part of the island, and the drops are only drawn for a camera under it. Thin slanted streaks under the cloud, leaning with the wind, and a darker, wetter ground where it has just passed would show from the default zoom where it rains and where it rained. What the player feels: weather is a thing on the map they can aim a village or a fire at. What it adds: the wildfire's player can see a front coming and choose to wait for it. What it costs: S, view only, one instanced mesh; measured on a large island. Related: "Puddles and wet ground after rain" in the backlog.
