section: Backlog

- **A lake that comes alive** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-20; lens: the second loop, a change the player watches happen). Weed now grows only in water that has stood ten minutes, so a lake a spell makes starts as a bare bowl. That wait could be the start of a small story: after ten minutes the first clumps of weed, after twenty lily pads on its still edge, after thirty a few fish. What the player feels: the lake they made is alive, and coming back to it later shows that time passed; it rewards a Rain or a Flood cast early in a session. What it adds: a visible reason to keep water still (draining or freezing it resets the clock), and a hook for later spells that speed it up. Cost: the clock is already there (Terrain.wetSince, in memory, not saved); lily pads would read it in placeCovers, fish would need their own work. Nothing saved, no paid calls; an island loaded from a save counts its water as old. Claude.
