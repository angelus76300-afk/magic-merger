section: Backlog

- **Lava that breaks open where something falls in** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-19; lens: the promise an item's name makes). Lava now has a crust that drifts and cracks that glow, but nothing a player does touches it. When a creature, a rock or a spell's body lands in a pool, the crust could split round that spot for a few seconds: the plates there tip apart, a wider ring of bright cracks opens, and the night glow flares there with it, then the crust closes again. What the player feels: the lava is a living thing that answers, and a spell that throws things into a volcano shows where they went. What it adds: a reason to aim at lava, and a readable hit even from far away at night. Cost: a few points (place, time) passed to the lava shader as a small uniform array, the same way the darkness passes its lights; no texture, no pass. Claude.
