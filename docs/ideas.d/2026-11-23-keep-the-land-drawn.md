section: Backlog

- **The island opens as it was left** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-23; lens: the first ten minutes, what the player feels). Each time an island opens, its fine land is built again from nothing: a minute or more of blocky land on a phone, every visit, though most of it has not changed. Keep each chunk's finished mesh in the browser's own storage (IndexedDB) with a hash of the cells it was built from, and on opening show every chunk whose cells still match at once; only the chunks that changed while the island was away are built again. What the player feels: the island they left is there the moment they come back, as a place, not a loading screen. What it adds: the fill only on a new island or a changed corner, and no time lost to land nobody touched. Cost: no paid calls; some 20 to 60 MB of storage on a large island (measure it first; keep the chunks nearest the camera when the browser gives less), and a check that the mesher's version and the looks are part of the hash, so a new look never shows an old mesh. Claude.
