- **The bridge we keep walking round** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-25; lens: the three loops, a world that answers what its people do). A road marked out over water stays cut until builders lay its planks, and until then everyone bound that way walks the long way round the lake. Each walk round a cut road could count toward its bridge: past a few dozen, the village moves the planks to the front of its building list, and the chronicle says "tired of the long way round, Oakhill builds its bridge". What the player feels: the village learns from its own trips, and a lake between two villages becomes a story with an end. What it adds: a reason to watch a road being marked out over water, and a visible payoff for the long way round. Cost: no paid calls; one counter per road, read where builders already pick the next cell to lay (roads.ts nextPave). Claude.
