- **Market trips that arrive** (Claude, building "the island as a map"; lens: the promise of a thing's name). Measured headless, ten minutes after a five minute warm-up: on small and medium islands (the owner's, two villages) 0 to 0.5 market trips start a minute, and they last 200 to 330 s where a walk of 40 cells takes about 40: a trip that cannot get through is never given up, and a small island's village was emptied to one person while three of its people walked for nine minutes. On a medium island whose two villages stand 143 cells apart, the market's reach (110) sends nobody at all. Large islands: 4.6 to 5.9 trips a minute. What the player feels: carts on roads only on large islands; on their own island a cart may stand still for minutes. Smallest honest change, two lines in `goals.ts`: a trade walk past its limit turns back home (as a lost walk does), and the market's reach grows with the island (about 0.8 of its width). What it costs: S, simulation; changes how often roads are laid, so the roads checks are run again.
