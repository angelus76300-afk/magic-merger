- **Houses that wear their village** (the builder of "Looks: village houses and trees in small cubes"; lens: what the player feels). A house in small cubes now has room for small signs of life, so let them follow the village: washing on a line when the weather is dry, firewood stacked high before winter and low after it, the flower boxes gone grey after a fire, a lantern lit by the door of a house whose villager is out at night. What the player feels: the village lives between their casts, and a glance at it tells them how it is doing. What it adds: a reason to zoom in on a village, and feedback for spells that help or harm it, with no text. What it costs: M, no paid call; render only, read from what the village already knows (season, weather, its people), one rebuild of a house model when its state changes.
