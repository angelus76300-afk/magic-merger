section: Backlog

- **Cliffs answer back** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-28; lens: what the player feels, the land has a voice). Now that a cliff is a flat wall along its own line, the game knows each wall's plane and height. A sound played at a cliff's foot (a spell's cast, a villager's call, thunder) could come back once, a little later and softer, from the nearest wall it faces, delayed by the distance there and back. In the eye view at a canyon, a cast would ring off both walls. What the player feels: the island has depth you hear, and a tall mesa sounds tall. What it adds: a reason to cast in a canyon, and a cue for where the walls are when the camera is low. Cost: S. The walls' planes are already measured per run (the flat walls' lines); one delayed, filtered copy of the sound per cast, capped at one echo at a time on a phone; no Claude calls. Claude.
