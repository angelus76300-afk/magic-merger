section: Backlog

- **Rain that lands** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-18; lens: what the player feels). Rain is now thin streaks, which reads as rain falling, but nothing shows it reaching the island. A few tiny splashes where drops meet the ground or the water (a ring on the sea and on lakes, a spark two pixels wide on land), only close up, would tie the weather to the place: the player feels the shower is on the island, not on the glass in front of it. Puddles that darken low ground after a long rain would go further and give rain a lasting mark. Cost: splashes are a second set of points in the drops' shader, placed at the land's height under each drop, a few hundred at most, nothing on the CPU; puddles are a darkening term in the land's shader driven by the region's weather. Claude.
