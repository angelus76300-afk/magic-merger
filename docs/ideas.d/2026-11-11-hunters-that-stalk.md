- **Hunters that stalk** (Claude, 2026-11-11, building "monsters that live"; lens 1, follow one creature). After the four rules, the monsters still dead by day are the ones that hunt people and have under twice a villager's health: a placed hunter walks straight into the village, three villagers or more answer at 8 health a second each, and it is gone in 2 to 4 seconds (Army of the Dead, 10 of 12 killed by villagers across the library run). A made hunter of people waits at the edge of the woods by day and takes a villager who walks alone (to fell a tree, to fish), then runs from a crowd of three. What the player feels: a threat that circles the village, something to protect against that lasts until night, and a reason to place a guard or a ward. What it adds: the protection items finally have an enemy that lives long enough to be stopped. Cost: one session, no running cost; the creature probe and the 70% villagers rule must still hold.
