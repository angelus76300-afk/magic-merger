- **A village's name says what it lost** (Claude, building "the island as a map"; lens: feedback, reading a place from the sky). From the whole island the player now reads names, roads, glow and smoke, but a village that lost half its people looks the same by day as one that never had them. The proposal: the name itself carries the change, its letters dimmed for a minute after a death in the village, and a town that fell to a village size losing its capitals the moment it does. No glyph and no number: the map only redraws what the village is. What the player feels: a loss is seen from far, and the next fire is a threat to a name they know. What it adds: the trouble's stakes on the map, between troubles. What it costs: S, view only, from `kin.ts` deaths already kept. Risk: a dimmed name is easy to miss at 344 wide; try it on the five reading seeds before building.
