- **A lake that tells its depth** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-26; lens: what the player feels, the island has secrets under its water). Now that a lake's floor fades from light to dark with its depth, a villager fishing from a boat or a dock could now and then call out what they saw below ("a sunken stone ring near the middle", "the deep is darker than a moonless night"), and a deep lake's darkest point could hold one rare find a spell can raise. What the player feels: the dark middle of a lake is a place, not a texture; it invites a look and a spell. What it adds: a reason to zoom in on water, and a small mystery on islands with deep lakes. Cost: no paid calls; a lake's deepest cell found once at the island's making (the lake checks already find it), a handful of story lines, one find placed there. Claude.
