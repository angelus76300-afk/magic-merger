- **A sundial in each village square** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-21; lens: what the player feels). Now that shadows glide with the sun, a shadow is a clock you can watch. A small stone dial in the square, its pointer's shadow sweeping across marks for dawn, noon and dusk, would let a player read the hour at a glance without the HUD and turn the day cycle into something seen in the world. What the player feels: the island keeps its own time. Cost: one prop model placed near the village's well, no new system; the shadow is the sun's own. Nothing saved. Claude.
