section: Gameplay

- **Glide from a height** (Claude, 12 November, after driving creatures in the eye view's second pass). A walker that runs off a cliff of three cells or more spreads its arms and glides: it keeps its run speed forward, sinks about one cell for every four it travels, and the stick steers it gently. It lands where the glide meets the ground, hurt by the fall as the engine already counts it. What the player feels: the hill they just climbed becomes a launch, and the island's height becomes something to use, not only something that stops them ("Too steep"). What it adds: a reason to climb, a fast way down to a village across a valley, and a moment of flight for a villager, not only for birds. Cost: S to M. It lives in `drive.ts` (only the ridden creature, so nothing else on the island changes or draws a different random number). It needs one more frame state for the falling villager and one Node check: the glide lands within 20 percent of a fall of four to one. No API call, nothing saved. Risk: a player glides onto the rim of the world, so the rim check in `drive.ts` must hold in the air too. Claude.
