# Ashes that wait

**Idea:** a Phoenix does not rise at once. It leaves a glowing heap of ash where it fell, and after about ten seconds it rises from it, unless a villager or the player walks into the ash first and scatters it for good (a small ember item as the reward).

**What the player feels:** the promise of the name, a bird that comes back, becomes a small drama. Wait and watch, or break it. The player chooses between a pet that never ends and a trophy.

**What it adds:** one more reason to look at a creature that dies, and a built in answer to an immortal creature that annoys a player, with no new menu.

**What it costs:** a timed corpse state in the engine, one particle effect, a test. No change to stored programs. It builds on the once per death rule of this fix.
