- 2026-11-13: **A golden hour worth stopping for** (Visual; builds on 5.2.5 sun
  shadows; lens: the player feels a time of day, not a clock): the sun now
  casts, follows the hour, and warms what it lights. Push the dusk and dawn
  further than the rest of the day. For the few minutes of 0.70 to 0.76 and
  0.95 to 1.0 in the cycle (daylight.ts), lengthen the shadows (a lower sun
  angle), deepen the warm tint and cool the shade more, and let the haze catch
  the light so the far side of the island glows. Cost: a few numbers in the sun
  and grade, no new pass. What it adds: a reason to open the World at the quiet
  hours, and a frame worth a screenshot (which is how a game like this spreads).
  What it costs the player: nothing; it is the same island, lit as the
  references are. Risk: a tint that reads as a bug if overdone, so tune it on
  frames at the five sizes before it ships.
