# A quiet sign when the game trims its looks

When a busy phone gives up shadows or the soft blur to keep its frames on time (the relief added on 16 November), the looks menu could say "shadows paused while the island is busy". The player then knows the change is on purpose and that it will come back, instead of thinking the game got worse. It costs one line in the menu, and it reads `TRIM.level`, which already exists.
