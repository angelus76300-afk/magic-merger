section: Living world

- **Raiders go for the log pile** (the builder of 4.9 part 2; lens: what can go wrong for the creatures). A building site's wood lies in the open: goblins and thieves steal logs from it at night, and a site robbed of its logs waits until the village pays again. Guards stand by a site after dark once one was robbed. What the player feels: a village is weakest while it grows, and a ward or a guardian creature cast by a site keeps the new house going up. What it adds: sites become something to protect, and the steal goal of 3.6 gets a target players can see shrink. What it costs: S, half a session (`steal` already takes from villages; a site's `logs` is the pile); no paid call. Risk: sites stuck for good; one repayment from the stores, then the site is given up. Claude.
- **Spells lend a hand** (the game designer, for 4.9 part 2; lens: the player and the village build together). A spell cast on a building site moves it on by its nature: earth or stone lays the footing, growth raises a timber frame, wind scatters the log pile. One stage per site per cast at most. What the player feels: their magic joins the village's own work. What it costs: S, if it reads what spells already declare (check `reactions` first); no paid call. Claude.
