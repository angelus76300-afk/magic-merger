- **Mountain goats on the ledges** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-27; lens: what the player feels, the land is lived on). Once cliffs follow their own line (the cliff outline prototype), their strata bands and lips run as long ledges instead of whole-cell notches. A few goats could walk those ledges a voxel or two wide, stop at a corner, jump down a step where two ledges meet, and stand on a lip looking out. What the player feels: a cliff is a place, not a wall; the eye view at a cliff's foot has something to watch, a reason to zoom in close. What it adds: life on the plateaus and mesas the owner looks at most from above, and a creature that shows how tall a cliff is by how small it is on it. Cost: M. A ledge graph built from the outline (the drawn lip and bands, the same for the sim's walker and the drawing, so a goat never stands in the air), a goat kind with a short hop, a check that it never leaves the graph; no Claude calls.
