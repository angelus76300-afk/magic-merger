section: Backlog

- **Light through the leaves** (Claude, while building "The island seen from the ground"; lens: what the player feels). In the eye view, under a crown at midday, a few bright flecks of sun on the ground move a little with the wind, and the trunk on the sun's side is a shade lighter. What the player feels: standing in a wood is a place, not a set of boxes, and the time of day reads from the ground, not only from the sky. What it adds: a reason to walk into the woods with no task, and a quiet cue that wind is blowing (the same wind that carries fire). What it costs: S to M, no paid call, nothing saved; drawn only within the near ring of trees (12 cells), as the near trees are, so the sky view pays nothing. The measure: seconds spent in the eye view per Become, in woods against open ground.
