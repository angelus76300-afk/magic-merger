- **Wheel ruts on busy roads** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-21; lens: what the player feels, the promise a place makes). Roads now lie on the ground in eighths, so they can carry marks. Each cart that passes darkens two thin lines an eighth wide along the road's middle, a little more with each trip, fading slowly when no cart comes. What the player feels: a road between two trading villages looks worn and used, a new one looks fresh, so trade shows close up without anything that changes with zoom. What it adds: a reason to look at roads, and a quiet sign of which villages trade. Cost: drawing only, a byte a road cell for the wear in the view (not saved, rebuilt from the trips over a few minutes), read in the fine land's colour pass; no paid calls. The risk is chunks rebuilt too often on a busy road: step the wear in four levels, so a chunk is built again only when a level changes. Claude.
