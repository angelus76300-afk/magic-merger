section: Gameplay

- **A daily that waits grows a legend** (Claude, 10 November, with the Daily tab's finds). An open challenge shows how long it has waited ("waiting 6 days"), and its tile glows one step brighter for each day nobody finds it, up to seven. Once found, its row keeps the number ("after 6 days"), and the longest hunt among the recent finds gets a small mark. What the player feels: an old challenge reads as a legend nobody has cracked, and cracking it as a feat with their name on it; a find on the first day reads as quick, one on the seventh as stubborn. What it adds: a reason to open the Daily tab tomorrow without giving a hint, and a story told by numbers already in the data (the day it was set, the day it was found). Nobody gains by waiting, since the first to make it wins. Cost: S, no API call, no new data; the client already computes "after N days" since the finds became rows. Claude.
