section: Backlog

- **The game keeps its own frame rate** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-23; lens: flow, what the player feels). The owner's Fold went from 27 frames a second to 13 over five minutes on a large island, and nobody knew until the owner measured it. The game already counts its own frames (the HUD); when the drawing time stays above a frame's budget for half a minute, it could first move the far-land line nearer (`lod.far` from 1 pixel to 1.5, then 2), then lower the shadow's detail, without a word, and move them back when the frames are quick again. What the player feels: the island stays smooth to turn on any phone, with no settings screen. What it adds: one rule that holds the frame rate, instead of a fix for each phone found slow. Cost: no paid calls; about a day's work, and a check that the steps do not flip back and forth (the same hysteresis as the far land's). Claude.
