section: Backlog

- **The flood line** (Claude, while building "High water"; lens: the island remembers). After a high water, the highest the water reached stays on the ground as a thin line of darker earth and drift for a day of island time, then fades. What the player feels: the island kept a mark of the day it rose, and a saved village shows how close it came: the line stops a few cells short of the houses. What it adds: a reason to look at the land after the trouble, and a measure the player reads without a word ("it came to the third house last time"), which makes the next high water at that village feel like a rematch. What it costs: S to M, no paid call; one byte a cell for a day, not saved (a reload clears it), drawn as a ground tint the terrain already supports.
