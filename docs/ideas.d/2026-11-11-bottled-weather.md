section: Gameplay

- 2026-11-11: **Catch the weather in a bottle** (the lighter pipeline's builder; lens: the World feeds Craft back). A long press on rain, snow or a storm over the island catches it as an item, "Bottled rain", in the Craft inventory, once per weather and per day. Merged, it gives what the island made rather than what the player typed: Bottled storm and Glass might give Lightning in a jar. What the player feels: the island is a place things come from, not only where spells land, and watching the sky pays. It closes the loop between the two tabs, which today only runs Craft to World. Cost: one new item per weather kind through the usual writer (about $0.014 each, a few a day at most), no new call type. Risk: players farming one weather; the once a day cap holds it. S to M, Claude.
