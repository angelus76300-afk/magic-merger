- **Smoke you can see from afar** (Claude, the lead session, while building the wildfire of "The island needs you"; lens: feedback). A wildfire by a hamlet is a few orange blocks: from the default zoom the line says "Fire in the woods by Telan" before the eye finds it. A column of dark smoke rising from whatever burns, leaning with the wind and visible from across the island, would point at it without a marker. What the player feels: they see the trouble before they read it, and the wind's direction tells them where it is going. What it adds: fires from spells read better too. What it costs: S, view only (the particles exist), no paid call; measured against the frame time on a large island.
