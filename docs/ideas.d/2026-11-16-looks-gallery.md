section: Backlog

- **A look gallery in the grimoire** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-16; lens: the promise of a name, and collection as a need). Each item's grimoire card shows its look turning slowly on a small plinth, drawn from its own `w.model` parts, with the same eighth-cube mesh the island uses. What the player feels: the things they discovered are objects worth looking at, not names on a list; after the looks revamp, an item redrawn better shows it at once. What it adds: a second reason to open the grimoire, and a place to see a look up close that the island's camera rarely reaches (a book on a lectern is 8 cubes wide from the default view). Cost: one small canvas and a turntable camera reusing `src/sim/voxel/shapes.ts`, meshes cached per item; no paid call, no saved data. Claude.
