- **Embers in the ruins, and the neighbours who help** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-23; lens: what the player feels, the second loop). Now that a burned house stands in ruins for a night, its rubble keeps a few glowing embers after dark, and at dawn the villagers of the next houses come over with the builders, each carrying a beam. What the player feels: the fire mattered; the village takes care of its own, and the morning is a small event worth watching (or watching for, with Watch). What it adds: a reason to come back to a village the morning after a mistake, and a softer side to Fire, the first spell a player has. Cost: drawing only for the embers (a faint glow on rubble cells whose `ruinedAt` is under a day old), a task of a few lines for the helpers (goals.ts repair, from neighbours within 6 cells); nothing saved, no paid calls.
