- **Cairns where someone froze** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-21; lens: goals leave marks). A creature that freezes to death now stands as a frost statue for 30 seconds, then is gone, and the pass looks as if nothing happened. Instead, the next villager or explorer who walks by could raise a small cairn on the spot, and people would keep a little further from it after: the mountain remembers who it took, and the player reads a dangerous pass at a glance. What it adds: a story told by the land, and a soft warning that keeps others off the coldest ways. Cost: the cairn itself exists (goals.ts), but "someone froze here" must outlive the body and the session, so it needs saved data (a mark kind kept in the save), which needs the owner's yes; a rule in wander() and the path cost to shy from such marks.
