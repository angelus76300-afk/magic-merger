section: Quality of life

- **The island stays put when the phone unfolds** (a builder's idea, 2026-11-10; lens: the owner's own device, a Z Fold). Opening a Fold mid-game turns a 344 wide screen into a 700 wide one in a second. If the camera keeps its corner instead of its center, the creature the player was watching jumps off to one side, and a drag in progress may end on the wrong cell. On a resize, keep the world point under the screen's center (or under a finger already down) at the same place, and finish or cancel a drag cleanly. What the player feels: unfolding is opening a book at the same page. What it adds: the bigger screen becomes something to reach for during play, not only before it. What it costs: S, the view's resize handler and one test that resizes 344 to 700 with a creature centered; no paid call. Claude.
