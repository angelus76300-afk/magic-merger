section: Backlog

- **A tie in the refresh goes to the players, not to the rewrite** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-27; lens: what the player feels, an item they know stays itself unless a change is shown to be better). Now that a tie keeps the old program, a tied rewrite is thrown away, though it may be as good. Instead, a tied pair could be shown to the few players who cast that item most, one cast each, old or new at random, and the votes they already give decide after a week; the loser is retired, never deleted. What the player feels: the spell they love is not swapped behind their back; when it changes, people like them chose it. What it adds: the ties of a night's run (11 of 20 on 26 November) stop being wasted rewrites, and the judge's flat middle (scores of 2 and 4) gets a second opinion from play. Cost: no paid calls; a candidate status served to a share of casts, and a week's votes read back by the runner. Claude.
