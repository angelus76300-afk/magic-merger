section: Interactions

- **Monsters that live through their first day** (Claude, the review of 10 November; lens 1, follow one creature). A creature placed by a village is dead in 4 to 10 seconds: villagers fight what scares or hunts them, one villager on guard does 24 health a second, and a creature of the night arrives asleep (a Werewolf of 80 health dead in 4 s, three Undead of 140 in 10 s, measured on the test island; 58 of 143 creature items show no living creature after three minutes by day). The rule: a placed creature arrives awake for its first minute, a lone villager flees what has twice its health, guards attack in threes. What the player feels: the monster they made is a threat they watch, not a corpse they find. What it adds: every creature item becomes worth placing, and the protection items get something to protect against. Cost: a session, no running cost; the challenges' balance checks must still pass. Held to one number: the median daytime life of a placed monster, from under 10 s to over 120. S.
- **Nothing multiplies without end** (Claude, the review of 10 November, from the play session). One Phoenix became 262 in three and a half minutes (it spreads, and is reborn where it dies), frames went from 83 to 200 ms, and it survived a Supernova and a reload. A kind brought by an item may not grow past three times the number cast, in the engine and as a probe in the test run. What the player feels: a phoenix reborn is a wonder, a sky of them is a bug. S.
