- **A rope from the shore** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-25; lens: what the player feels, the villagers look after each other). When a villager has been swimming for the shore for a while, a neighbour on the bank could walk to the water's edge and throw a rope, and the two pull it in together, with a line in the island's stories ("Bryn pulled Edda out of the lake"). What the player feels: the people of the island notice each other, and a flood becomes a small story instead of a silent loss. What it adds: a reason to watch the water after a rain spell, and a bond between two named villagers. Cost: no paid calls; one task in goals.ts for the helper (walk to the nearest dry cell next to the swimmer, then tow it a few cells), a rope drawn as a thin line between two hands, and a check that the swimmer is ashore sooner with a helper than without. Claude.
