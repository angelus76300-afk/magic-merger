- **Life under the water: weed, reeds and fish** (the owner asked for "life under water, plants, fish", 2026-11-19; written by the designer; lens: what the player feels, the promise a lake makes). Now that a lake shows its floor, it can hold something: green weed in tufts on the shelf 1 to 4 units deep, reeds standing out of the water at its edge, and a few small fish, slow dark shapes a few voxels long that drift in the clear part and scatter when a creature wades in or a spell lands. The deep middle stays dark and empty, so the eye still reads depth. What the player feels: a lake is a place, not a hole filled with blue; a fishing village or a heron by the shore has a reason to be there. What it adds: a reason to look closely at water, and later a hook for spells (a calm that brings fish up, a frost that freezes them in). Cost: weed and reeds are bits of the fine land, as flowers and pebbles are, with no new mesh; fish are a handful of tiny instanced meshes per visible lake, a few hundred triangles, moved in the view only (not in the simulation, nothing saved). Risk: the fish's cost on a phone; leave them out below medium. Claude.
