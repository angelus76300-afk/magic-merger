section: Gameplay

- **Sounds through the mist** (Claude, 12 November, after cutting the eye view's draw distance to 80 cells). The mist now hides the far island, so let the ear reach past it: a village beyond the mist is heard as a low murmur of voices and hammers from its direction, a fire as a crackle, a wolf pack as a howl, each panned left or right by where it lies against the look and quieter with distance. What the player feels: the island goes on past what they can see, and they turn toward a sound to find something. What it adds: a reason to walk somewhere in the eye view, and a cheap hint of troubles (a wildfire heard before it is seen) that points the player at the item that answers it. Cost: S. The sound engine already plays positional cues (`sound.ts`); this picks at most three sources past 60 cells every second, from the villages, fires and packs the simulation already lists. No API call, nothing saved, no new draw. Risk: noise; cap it to three quiet sources and only while in the eye view. Claude.
