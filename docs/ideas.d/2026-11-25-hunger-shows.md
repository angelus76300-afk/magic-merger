section: Backlog

- **Hunger you can see** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-25; lens: what the player feels, the promise a figure makes). Today a villager can go hungry for minutes, and the only sign is a word in its card. Give a hungry villager a sign the player sees without tapping: past 0.9 hunger it walks a little slower and a small empty bowl floats over it, as the sleeping "z" does. What the player feels: they notice a village in trouble (cut off by a flood, its stores burned) before people die, and can help with a spell: a bridge, a drained lake, a rain of berries. What it adds: a reason to cast that comes from the villagers, not from a challenge, and a death seen coming instead of a surprise in the news. Cost: no paid calls; a flag on the villager's draw state from its hunger, one sprite, and frames at the five sizes. Claude.
