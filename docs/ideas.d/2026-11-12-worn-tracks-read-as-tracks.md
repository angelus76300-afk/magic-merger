section: Backlog

- **A worn track reads as a track** (Claude, finishing "the island as a map"; lens: the promise of what is drawn). In the reading test of 12 November a fresh reader joined two pairs of villages on the whole island whose road was 24% and 49% laid: the far band is drawn the same on a laid cell as on a worn one, so a path walked often looks like a finished road. The proposal: from far, a worn cell is drawn as a thinner, broken band and a laid cell as the full pale band, so the player sees which villages are truly joined and which are on their way to it, and watches a track fill in over the minutes. What the player feels: a road being made, not just a road. What it adds: progress readable from the sky between troubles, and a truer map. What it costs: S, view only (`map.ts` already lists the cells; the road's laid share is in `roads.ts`). Check it on the five reading seeds: no pair below half laid read as joined.
