section: Backlog

- **Neighbours come to help** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-25; lens: what the player feels, the social loop). Today a family with no builder near rebuilds its burned house alone. When a village loses two or more houses at once, send one builder from the nearest other village of the island along the road for the morning, with a news line ("Builders from Morbrook came to help rebuild Halwick"), and have it walk home at dusk. What the player feels: the villages of an island know each other, and a disaster brings them together; the road between them gets used. What it adds: a reason for roads between villages, and a story after a fire or a flood. Cost: no paid calls; a few lines in goals.ts build() (a ruin of another village of the island, nearest first, only when its own village has none), and a check that the builder is back in its own village by the evening. Claude.
