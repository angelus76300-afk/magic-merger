- **Skating on frozen lakes** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-20; lens: the promise a thing makes, what the player feels). A frozen tundra lake now reads as one smooth sheet, with villagers walking to its edge. On ice they could slide: walkers keep a little speed when they stop and turn wider, and a villager in a long idle sometimes skates a loop on a big frozen lake. What the player feels: winter on the island is a season people live in, not only a colour; a Freeze cast on a lake gives the village a place to play. What it adds: a reason to cast cold spells near villages, and a small scene the Watch camera can find. Cost: movement only (creatures.ts already lets walkers onto ice at full speed); no saved data, no paid calls. The risk is a villager sliding off the edge into open water, which steering must refuse. Claude.
