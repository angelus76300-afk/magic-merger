- **Footprints across sand and snow** (the designer's idea, 2026-11-21; lens: what the player feels, the promise a place makes). The ground now blends where two lands meet, so a dune field reads as one surface; that leaves room for marks on it. A villager or animal walking on dry sand or snow leaves a faint darker trail of eighth-size dents that fades over a few minutes, sooner in wind or a snowfall. What the player feels: the island is lived in between spells, and the Watch camera can follow a trail to a herd. What it adds: life on the plain grounds with the least to look at, the desert and the tundra. Cost: drawing only, in the fine land's colour pass near the camera (a small ring of recent steps a chunk); no saved data, no paid calls. The risk is cost in a busy village: cap the steps kept a chunk, and none on low. Claude.
