# Seasons painted on the cubes

Now that the whole island is one size of small cube, a season can be painted on it without a new mesh: the grass's soft tone patches turn gold in a dry spell, the trees' top shade turns orange in autumn, and after a cold night the highest steps of each slope turn white first, so frost creeps down the hills in the island's own contour lines. What the player feels: the island they watch for an hour is not the one they started with; a hill they know looks different on the way back. What it adds: a reason to zoom out and look at the whole island, which until now looked the same at every visit. Cost: a colour pass over the tone patches and the tree shades, rebuilt a chunk at a time over a minute (the land is already built that way); no new triangles, no data saved beyond the day count, no money.
