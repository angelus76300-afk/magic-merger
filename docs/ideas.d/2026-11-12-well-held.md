section: Backlog

- **The well held** (the game designer, while building "High water", for the later drought; lens: what the player feels). A village with a standing well gets the line "Ashby's well held through the dry spell" when a dry spell passes it by, with no trouble and no stone. What the player feels: a past invention still working, the island remembering without being asked. What it adds: a quiet payoff between troubles, and a reason to look at a village that is fine. What it costs: S, no paid call, nothing saved (the well is already a cover; the line is one story line).
