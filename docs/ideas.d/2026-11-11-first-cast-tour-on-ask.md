section: Quality of life

- **The wonders' tour on the player's word** (a builder's idea, 2026-11-11; lens: the first ten minutes). Since the first cast fix the tour of a new island waits for the first cast and starts itself after eight quiet seconds. A camera that leaves on its own is still a surprise, and a player who was watching a creature loses it. Instead, after the first cast, the island's name card could sit at the bottom with one button, "Show the wonders", and play the tour only when tapped; the card goes away after twenty seconds. What the player feels: the island is theirs and the tour is a gift they open, not something done to them. What it adds: a reason to look at the wonders (they are where wonder casts and stories happen) without taking the camera. What it costs: S, the frame's tour trigger and one button in world.html; no paid call. Measure with scripts/first-cast.mjs plus the share of players who tap it. Claude.
